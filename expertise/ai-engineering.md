# AI Engineering

My view: AI is most valuable when it is **engineered into the delivery system** — with clear boundaries, budgets, and evidence — not bolted on as a demo.

I use AI in two complementary ways:

1. **AI-assisted development** — Spec-Driven Design, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and Claude Code to raise the quality and speed of design and implementation while keeping humans accountable for architecture and review.
2. **AI-powered systems** — LangChain, LangGraph, RAG, and cloud LLM services to build assistants and workflows that answer real engineering questions under explicit constraints (call limits, source validation, traceability).

I prefer prototypes that show **orchestration, failure modes, and cost control**, not only happy-path prompts. The [Engineering Knowledge Assistant](../showcase_engineering_knowledge_assistant.md) is the concrete expression of that approach.
